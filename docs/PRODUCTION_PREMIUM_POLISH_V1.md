# Toolsiva Production Premium Polish v1

This package upgrades the site shell before public launch.

## Included

- Complete Privacy Policy and Terms of Use pages.
- Disclaimer, Contact and Accessibility pages.
- Premium multi-column footer.
- Automatic copyright year range.
- Configurable contact email.
- Expanded Open Graph and Twitter metadata.
- Default social sharing image.
- Canonical, robots and richer metadata.
- WebSite and Organization JSON-LD.
- Keyboard skip link.
- Premium homepage with category counts and trust section.
- Improved About page.
- Production 404 page.
- Sitemap entry in robots.txt.
- Editorial styles for legal and information pages.

## Before launch

Confirm that `hello@toolsiva.com` exists or replace `contactEmail` in
`src/config/site.ts`.

The included legal text is a general operational template and is not a
substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer for a specific jurisdiction,
business model, analytics setup or advertising setup.
